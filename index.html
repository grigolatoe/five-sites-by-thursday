Thursday: five sites, and how sure we are

If it works for you, I'd bring these five. Each one is scored with the rubric agreed with the sponsor. Two are clean; three carry one open point each, and I think it's better that the sponsor hears those from us.

  1. Grelle Harbour Plot (S-018, Nordholm): 97. Letter of intent signed, council minutes record support, 4.6 MW spare grid capacity 0.8 km away. Nothing open.
  2. Kessby Old Quarry Yard (S-003, Aldmark): 97. Letter of intent signed, council support. The owner wants a 12% revenue share; it affects the sponsor's numbers, so I'd mention it.
  3. Brekke Depot (S-017/S-031, Sveld): 96. Letter of intent signed on 8 Sep. It was logged twice in our tracker; it is one plot.
  4. Varne Farm Edge (S-027, Tarrow): 93. The best grid connection of all 40, and a signed letter. The council is waiting for a noise report before taking a position.
  5. Orlund Depot Yard (S-014, Sveld): 86. Strong grid, and the ward councillor is in favour. The owner is still in talks, with no letter yet.

Reserve, if one drops: Ashcombe Field Corner (S-032, 82). As strong as Orlund, but nobody has recorded what the council thinks.

What I'm confident about

  • Every grid figure is the official one, the source the sponsor trusts. None comes from the vendor's unvalidated estimate.
  • Every owner and council position traces back to a dated sentence from the team's field notes.
  • None of the five is in a flood-prone or protected area.

What I'm not sure about

  • The noise report at Varne Farm Edge could turn the council either way.
  • The scores are only as good as the data they rest on. Eight sites couldn't be scored on grid capacity, because the national feed timed out on the day of the pull. One of them, Hollin Road (S-011), already has a signed letter and could well belong in the top five.

Why some obvious-looking sites aren't there

  • Esk Mill Land (S-009) scores a perfect 100. But on 10 Sep the municipal ecologist said a third of it falls inside a reserve designated in 2024, and our land record dates from 2018. I've held it back until that's checked.
  • Ulven Works (S-006), among others: Nordholm publishes capacity in kW, not MW. Read as MW, 1,700 looks perfect; it's actually 1.7 MW, too little for a 2 MW battery.
  • Heron Marsh (S-033) is inside a protected nature area, which the agreed rule excludes.
  • Brannoch Depot (S-020): the owner refused on 5 Sep.
  • Rook Street (S-013): half the plot was sold for housing last year. About 700 m² remains, which is too small, and new homes next door are likely neighbours to object.
  • Pellick Lane (S-024) and Gorse Hill (S-036) look good on the grid, but the land registry has no record of them and nobody knows the owner yet.

The quick method first proposed (fill gaps with averages, take numbers as published) would have put Ulven Works and Esk Mill Land in the top five.

What I'd do next

  • Re-run the grid pull for the eight sites that timed out. It takes minutes and could change the list.
  • Ask the ecologist about Esk Mill Land. If it's clear, it goes straight to the top.
  • Before a study is commissioned, confirm the noise report at Varne Farm Edge and the letter at Orlund.
